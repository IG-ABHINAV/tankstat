# Fuel log, consumption and statistics

## Fuel log and statistics

- Domain: fuel log. `Vehicle` and `Refueling` entities have private setters and `Create` factories that enforce the rules (throwing `DomainException`); Application services (`VehicleService`, `RefuelingService`, `ResourceSharingService`, `ImageService`) use repository ports; the GraphQL error filter maps `DomainException` / `NotFoundException` to `VALIDATION_FAILED` / `NOT_FOUND` errors.
- A discount (#204): `Cost.Discount` is what the till took off, `Cost.Amount` stays what was paid, so every sum of spending, the key figures and the Fuel cost / Fuel price charts count what was paid; only `AmountBeforeDiscount` and a refuelling's `PricePerUnitBeforeDiscount` (the pump's price, next to `PricePerUnit`, the price paid) add it back, and the one chart metric that does is `AveragePricePerUnitBeforeDiscount` (non-additive, like the paid one). A discount is never negative (`cost.discountNegative`, said before anything else), is kept to the cent (what rounds to 0 is none on every provider: the column is `(14,2)`, SQLite would keep more), needs the amount it was taken off unless it is none (`cost.discountWithoutAmount`: a log left for its photos gets one only from the photo, with the total it creates, `Refueling.FillFromPhoto`), and an update that leaves `discount` out (or null) keeps the log's, like `missedPreviousFillUp` (a build from before discounts, still open after an upgrade, sends none): 0 clears it, which the edit dialog sends for an emptied field (`null` on an add), and `KEEP_WHEN_NULL.refuelings` holds it. The dialog's amounts (`refuelingAmounts.ts` `keepAmountsInStep`, the order in `.claude/rules/photo-reading.md`): total = volume × unit price − discount; the discount is worked out only from all three others (the litres, the amount paid and the pump's price typed or read give what the till took off; never below nothing, and a difference under 0.1 % of the pump's amount is the receipt's rounding, not a discount), an empty one is none.
- Fuel consumption (`Refueling.Consumption`, fuel per 100 distance units between full fill-ups) is stored, not computed on read: `ConsumptionCalculator` (pure, Domain) recomputes a vehicle's whole chain and returns only the logs whose value changed; `RefuelingService` calls it after every log/update/delete/restore (`RecalculateConsumptionAsync`; imports pass `recalculateConsumption: false` and recalculate once; the seeder applies it directly) and `DatabaseMigrator` backfills once after the `AddRefuelingConsumption` migration. A log with `MissedPreviousFillUp` (a fill-up before it was not logged; GraphQL `missedPreviousFillUp`, omitted on update = kept) breaks the chain: its interval has no consumption and the next one starts from it. Anything that changes logs, odometers, volumes, full-tank or missed flags must go through that path.
- Statistics (`Application/Stats`): `StatsCalculator` (pure) turns a vehicle's live records (`IStatsRepository.LoadAsync`) into chart data and key figures; costs are always per currency, never converted, and drawn on a scale per currency too (#159: `ChartView` gives every currency of a `CURRENCY` chart a y axis of its own, `dashboard/chartFormat.ts` `chartAxes`; see the frontend rule's Charts). The vehicle summary (card and dashboard key figures) has a main currency (most spent in over the last year) for `thisMonthSpend`/`lastMonthSpend`/`spendTrend`, plus `spending` (`CurrencySpend` per currency used this month or last, main first), which the UI shows joined (`spending.ts` `spentText`: "52,000 Ft · €73.90") so nothing spent in another currency goes unshown. A chart is a `ChartConfig` (metric, grouping, kind, range incl. custom from-to, stacked, and for a chart over time `Trend` (None/MovingAverage/Linear), `RunningTotal` (metrics that add up, never with a trend) and `CompareYearEarlier`; #211) that `Validated()` normalises (dates dropped for presets); saved ones are `VehicleChart` rows (typed columns, personal or shared, managed by `ChartService`). `StatsCalculator.Chart` works out the value series (`Measure`: costs by date inside the range, distance from the readings of the part of each period inside it), then the running total, then the year earlier (`Measure` over the range a year back, its keys moved a year on so they line up; a stacked chart compares each currency's total), then the trend per value series (per currency's total when stacked; a metric that adds up leaves out a period not over yet; moving average of 3, linear least squares from 3 points, `Slope` per period); every series carries its `Role` (`ChartSeriesRole`: Value, MovingAverage, Linear, YearEarlier), values first. `CostPerDistance`/`FuelCostPerDistance` (unit `CostPerDistance`, per currency, null without distance) are `Money` over `DistanceIn`. A chart over time can have a second measure (`SecondMetric` + `SecondKind`: Bar/Line/Area, another metric than the first; `chart.secondNeedsPeriods`/`secondSameMetric`/`secondNeedsKind`; a `SecondKind` without a metric is dropped): calculated after the first's lines, never stacked, no options of its own, so the series are the first's values, its year earlier and trends, then the second's values. `Series` stamps every series it returns with its `Metric` and `Unit` (one exit, the category chart too: the record's defaults are `TotalSpend`/`Currency`, so an unstamped series would be wrong); `ChartData.Unit` stays the first measure's. `ChartConfigInput`'s new fields are nullable (absent = none, for an older client). The frontend draws every series that is not a value as a line over the values (`chartFormat.ts` `isValue`, `ChartView`), and words a linear trend from its first value to its last (in the chart's units, so mpg reads right), and why a line asked for is missing. Presets live in the frontend (`dashboard/DashboardPanel.tsx`) and use the same `vehicleChartData` query. Add a metric = enum value + calculator case + i18n (`charts.metrics.*`) + builder rules in `dashboard/chartFormat.ts` (`normalise` mirrors `ChartConfig.Validated`).
