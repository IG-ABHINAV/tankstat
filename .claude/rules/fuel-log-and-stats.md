# Fuel log, consumption and statistics

## Fuel log and statistics

- Domain: fuel log. `Vehicle` and `Refueling` entities have private setters and `Create` factories that enforce the rules (throwing `DomainException`); Application services (`VehicleService`, `RefuelingService`, `ResourceSharingService`, `ImageService`) use repository ports; the GraphQL error filter maps `DomainException` / `NotFoundException` to `VALIDATION_FAILED` / `NOT_FOUND` errors.
- A discount (#204): `Cost.Discount` is what the till took off, `Cost.Amount` stays what was paid, so every sum of spending, the key figures and the Fuel cost / Fuel price charts count what was paid; only `AmountBeforeDiscount` and a refuelling's `PricePerUnitBeforeDiscount` (the pump's price, next to `PricePerUnit`, the price paid) add it back, and the one chart metric that does is `AveragePricePerUnitBeforeDiscount` (non-additive, like the paid one). A discount is never negative (`cost.discountNegative`, said before anything else), is kept to the cent (what rounds to 0 is none on every provider: the column is `(14,2)`, SQLite would keep more), needs the amount it was taken off unless it is none (`cost.discountWithoutAmount`: a log left for its photos gets one only from the photo, with the total it creates, `Refueling.FillFromPhoto`), and an update that leaves `discount` out (or null) keeps the log's, like `missedPreviousFillUp` (a build from before discounts, still open after an upgrade, sends none): 0 clears it, which the edit dialog sends for an emptied field (`null` on an add), and `KEEP_WHEN_NULL.refuelings` holds it. The dialog's amounts (`refuelingAmounts.ts` `keepAmountsInStep`, the order in `.claude/rules/photo-reading.md`): total = volume × unit price − discount; the discount is worked out only from all three others (the litres, the amount paid and the pump's price typed or read give what the till took off; never below nothing, and a difference under 0.1 % of the pump's amount is the receipt's rounding, not a discount), an empty one is none.
- Fuel consumption (`Refueling.Consumption`, fuel per 100 distance units between full fill-ups) is stored, not computed on read: `ConsumptionCalculator` (pure, Domain) recomputes a vehicle's whole chain and returns only the logs whose value changed; `RefuelingService` calls it after every log/update/delete/restore (`RecalculateConsumptionAsync`; imports pass `recalculateConsumption: false` and recalculate once; the seeder applies it directly) and `DatabaseMigrator` backfills once after the `AddRefuelingConsumption` migration. A log with `MissedPreviousFillUp` (a fill-up before it was not logged; GraphQL `missedPreviousFillUp`, omitted on update = kept) breaks the chain: its interval has no consumption and the next one starts from it. Anything that changes logs, odometers, volumes, full-tank or missed flags must go through that path.
- Statistics (`Application/Stats`): `StatsCalculator` (pure) turns a vehicle's live records (`IStatsRepository.LoadAsync`) into chart data and key figures; costs are always per currency, never converted, and drawn on a scale per currency too (#159: `ChartView` gives every currency of a `CURRENCY` chart a y axis of its own, `dashboard/chartFormat.ts` `chartAxes`; see the frontend rule's Charts). The vehicle summary (card and dashboard key figures) has a main currency (most spent in over the last year) for `thisMonthSpend`/`lastMonthSpend`/`spendTrend`, plus `spending` (`CurrencySpend` per currency used this month or last, main first), which the UI shows joined (`spending.ts` `spentText`: "52,000 Ft · €73.90") so nothing spent in another currency goes unshown. A chart is a `ChartConfig` (metric, grouping, kind, range incl. custom from-to, stacked, and for a chart over time `Trend` (None/MovingAverage/Linear), `RunningTotal` (metrics that add up, never with a trend) and `CompareYearEarlier`; #211) that `Validated()` normalises (dates dropped for presets); saved ones are `VehicleChart` rows (typed columns, personal or shared, managed by `ChartService`; its two rights are decided in one place, #210: `ChartService.MayChange` (the creator) and `MayDelete` (the creator, or Delete access to the logs), which the mutations refuse by (`chart.notYours`) and GraphQL's `VehicleChart.canEdit`/`canDelete` say, the level through `LogLevelByVehicleLoader` and `createdBy` through `UserRefLoader`, never a lookup per chart; the dashboard shows the pencil and the bin each by its own flag). `StatsCalculator.Chart` works out the value series (`Measure`: costs by date inside the range, distance from the readings of the part of each period inside it), then the running total, then the year earlier (`Measure` over the range a year back, its keys moved a year on so they line up; a stacked chart compares each currency's total), then the trend per value series (per currency's total when stacked; a metric that adds up leaves out a period that is not whole: not over yet, or cut by the range's edge; a linear line is floored at 0; moving average of 3, linear least squares from 3 points, `Slope` per period); every series carries its `Role` (`ChartSeriesRole`: Value, MovingAverage, Linear, YearEarlier), values first. `CostPerDistance`/`FuelCostPerDistance` (unit `CostPerDistance`, per currency, null without distance) are `Money` over `DistanceIn`. A fuel price (`AveragePricePerUnit`, the one before discounts) has the unit `PricePerVolume`, so beside a cost it gets a scale of its own and is written per litre. A chart over time can have a second measure (`SecondMetric` + `SecondKind`: Bar/Line/Area, another metric than the first; `chart.secondNeedsPeriods`/`secondSameMetric`/`secondNeedsKind`; a `SecondKind` without a metric is dropped): calculated after the first's lines, never stacked, no options of its own, so the series are the first's values, its year earlier and trends, then the second's values. `Series` stamps every series it returns with its `Metric` and `Unit` (one exit, the category chart too: the record's defaults are `TotalSpend`/`Currency`, so an unstamped series would be wrong); `ChartData.Unit` stays the first measure's. `ChartConfigInput`'s new fields are nullable (absent = none, for an older client). The frontend draws every series that is not a value as a line over the values (`chartFormat.ts` `isValue`, `ChartView`; a year earlier with nothing in it is not drawn, only listed in the table and said in a note), and words a linear trend from its first value to its last (in the chart's units, so mpg reads right), and why a line asked for is missing. The ready-made charts, the key figures and the layout presets live in the frontend (`dashboard/widgets.ts`, pure; the figures drawn by `dashboard/Figures.tsx`) and use the same `vehicleChartData` query. **The dashboard's arrangement** (#211): `DashboardLayoutValues` (tiles `DashboardTile(Id, X, Y, W, H, Pinned, Period)` in a four-column grid, ids a figure's, `preset:<id>` or `chart:<guid>`; `Period` a trend figure's own `TrendPeriod` (#230), null for its default, refused on any other tile (`dashboard.itemInvalid`) and checked by `Checked(today)` against the save's UTC day; the hidden saved charts; shape and bounds only (x + w ≤ 4, h ≤ 24, 60 per list, `dashboard.itemInvalid`/`tooMany`), never overlaps: the web app packs them) in `VehicleDashboardLayouts` (the vehicle's, Edit on its logs, `dashboard.needEditToChange`) and `UserDashboardLayouts` (one's own, View; keyed by user without a foreign key, deleted with the user), both cascading with the vehicle, the tiles as JSON text without a length cap; `DashboardService` drops a saved chart the layout's audience cannot see (another vehicle's, gone, a private one on the vehicle's), never refuses it; GraphQL `Vehicle.dashboard` (nullable, per vehicle: only `VehicleDashboard` selects it, never a list), `saveDashboardLayout`, `resetDashboardLayout`. Outside editing a tile shows only its content, its numbers in a dialog from the card header's button (`ChartTableDialog`). **The trend figures** (#211 D, #230): each looks at a period of its own. `Domain/Charts/TrendPeriod` (`Kind` `Last`/`Since`/`ThisYear`, `Unit` days/weeks/months/years/fill-ups, `Count` 1..`MaxCount` (1000), `Since` from `Earliest` (2000-01-01) to today, `Whole` only for weeks, months and years, `Against` `Before`/`YearEarlier`; enums stored by name; `Validated(today)` refuses rather than normalises: `trend.countRange`/`sinceRange`/`wholeNeedsUnit`/`invalid`); `ThisYear` (1 January to today) exists because the year so far cannot be stored as a date, which would freeze at the year it was saved. The defaults are the figures as they were (`TwelveWholeMonths` for spending and cost per distance, `TenFillUps`, `ThisYearAgainstLast` for distance). `StatsCalculator.Trend(data, figure, period, today, mainCurrency)` (`TrendFigureData`: the two `TrendWindow`s (start, end, fill-ups in them) and the figure's values) works out the windows (`Windows`): *Last N*, not whole, ends today; whole weeks end the Sunday before today, whole months and years with the last completed one; N fill-ups run from the oldest of the latest N (by date, then odometer; for consumption only those with a stored consumption) to today, the N before them up to the day before; *Before* is as long (calendar months or years for those units, else days) right before, *YearEarlier* both ends `AddYears(-1)` (a leap day lands on 28 February), `ThisYear` before is as many days ending 31 December; money per currency, the main currency first and always (also with nothing in the windows, so the figure headlines the card's currency), then those used in either window; cost per distance over `DistanceIn` of each window (null with nothing driven); consumption the mean of the stored consumptions in each window (over fill-ups the items' own), the recent as soon as there is one, the earlier from `MinTrendFillUps` (3), else null; distance null for a vehicle without a reading. `VehicleSummary.Trends` (`DashboardTrends`) is the four defaults through `Trend`, one code path, kept for an older client; the web app selects it nowhere. The Add widget dialog's trend figure pane (`TrendFigurePane`) has the same form under its preview tile, and adds the figure with its period (`addTile(…, period)`). `toLayout` sends a tile's period only when it has one of its own (`StoredTile`). The words are `dashboard.trend.{more,less,same}{,Fuel}` with `{{than}}` from `dashboard.trend.than.*` (plurals in both languages), the chip's `dashboard.period.*`; the labels name the figure only (*Spending*, *Cost per distance change*, *Consumption change*, *Distance driven*: never a ready-made chart's title, which the Add widget list holds too). `changeWords.ts` (`useChangeWords`, drawn by `Kpi.tsx`'s `ChangeLine` and `ChangeArrow`) words a change with an arrow and a per cent ("HUF 60,000 (12%) more than the 12 months before", consumption as uses more/less fuel, a window by dates adding the fill-ups compared), the arrow in a circle coloured by its `meaning` (#228: `worseUp` for spending, cost per distance and consumption, so up is `error` and down `success`; `neutral` for distance, `primary` either way; no change grey; `data-tone` for the tests), never the colour alone.
