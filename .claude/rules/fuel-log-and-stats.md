# Fuel log, consumption and statistics

## Fuel log and statistics

- Domain: fuel log. `Vehicle` and `Refueling` entities have private setters and `Create` factories that enforce the rules (throwing `DomainException`); Application services (`VehicleService`, `RefuelingService`, `ResourceSharingService`, `ImageService`) use repository ports; the GraphQL error filter maps `DomainException` / `NotFoundException` to `VALIDATION_FAILED` / `NOT_FOUND` errors.
- A discount (#204): `Cost.Discount` is what the till took off, `Cost.Amount` stays what was paid, so every sum of spending, the key figures and the Fuel cost / Fuel price charts count what was paid; only `AmountBeforeDiscount` and a refuelling's `PricePerUnitBeforeDiscount` (the pump's price, next to `PricePerUnit`, the price paid) add it back, and the one chart metric that does is `AveragePricePerUnitBeforeDiscount` (non-additive, like the paid one). A discount is never negative (`cost.discountNegative`, said before anything else), is kept to the cent (what rounds to 0 is none on every provider: the column is `(14,2)`, SQLite would keep more), needs the amount it was taken off unless it is none (`cost.discountWithoutAmount`: a log left for its photos gets one only from the photo, with the total it creates, `Refueling.FillFromPhoto`), and an update that leaves `discount` out (or null) keeps the log's, like `missedPreviousFillUp` (a build from before discounts, still open after an upgrade, sends none): 0 clears it, which the edit dialog sends for an emptied field (`null` on an add), and `KEEP_WHEN_NULL.refuelings` holds it. The dialog's amounts (`refuelingAmounts.ts` `keepAmountsInStep`, the order in `.claude/rules/photo-reading.md`): total = volume × unit price − discount; the discount is worked out only from all three others (the litres, the amount paid and the pump's price typed or read give what the till took off; never below nothing, and a difference under 0.1 % of the pump's amount is the receipt's rounding, not a discount), an empty one is none.
- Fuel consumption (`Refueling.Consumption`, fuel per 100 distance units between full fill-ups) is stored, not computed on read: `ConsumptionCalculator` (pure, Domain) recomputes a vehicle's whole chain and returns only the logs whose value changed; `RefuelingService` calls it after every log/update/delete/restore (`RecalculateConsumptionAsync`; imports pass `recalculateConsumption: false` and recalculate once; the seeder applies it directly) and `DatabaseMigrator` backfills once after the `AddRefuelingConsumption` migration. A log with `MissedPreviousFillUp` (a fill-up before it was not logged; GraphQL `missedPreviousFillUp`, omitted on update = kept) breaks the chain: its interval has no consumption and the next one starts from it. Anything that changes logs, odometers, volumes, full-tank or missed flags must go through that path.
- Statistics (`Application/Stats`): `StatsCalculator` (pure) turns a vehicle's live records (`IStatsRepository.LoadAsync`) into chart data and key figures; costs are always per currency, never converted, and drawn on a scale per currency too (#159: `ChartView` gives every currency of a `CURRENCY` chart a y axis of its own, `dashboard/chartFormat.ts` `chartAxes`; see the frontend rule's Charts). The vehicle summary (card and dashboard key figures) has a main currency (most spent in over the last year) for `thisMonthSpend`/`lastMonthSpend`/`spendTrend`, plus `spending` (`CurrencySpend` per currency used this month or last, main first), which the UI shows joined (`spending.ts` `spentText`: "52,000 Ft · €73.90") so nothing spent in another currency goes unshown. A chart is a `ChartConfig` (metric, grouping, kind, range incl. custom from-to, stacked, and for a chart over time `Trend` (None/MovingAverage/Linear), `RunningTotal` (metrics that add up, never with a trend) and `CompareYearEarlier`; #211) that `Validated()` normalises (dates dropped for presets); saved ones are `VehicleChart` rows (typed columns, personal or shared, managed by `ChartService`). `StatsCalculator.Chart` works out the value series (`Measure`: costs by date inside the range, distance from the readings of the part of each period inside it), then the running total, then the year earlier (`Measure` over the range a year back, its keys moved a year on so they line up; a stacked chart compares each currency's total), then the trend per value series (per currency's total when stacked; a metric that adds up leaves out a period that is not whole: not over yet, or cut by the range's edge; a linear line is floored at 0; moving average of 3, linear least squares from 3 points, `Slope` per period); every series carries its `Role` (`ChartSeriesRole`: Value, MovingAverage, Linear, YearEarlier), values first. `CostPerDistance`/`FuelCostPerDistance` (unit `CostPerDistance`, per currency, null without distance) are `Money` over `DistanceIn`. A fuel price (`AveragePricePerUnit`, the one before discounts) has the unit `PricePerVolume`, so beside a cost it gets a scale of its own and is written per litre. A chart over time can have a second measure (`SecondMetric` + `SecondKind`: Bar/Line/Area, another metric than the first; `chart.secondNeedsPeriods`/`secondSameMetric`/`secondNeedsKind`; a `SecondKind` without a metric is dropped): calculated after the first's lines, never stacked, no options of its own, so the series are the first's values, its year earlier and trends, then the second's values. `Series` stamps every series it returns with its `Metric` and `Unit` (one exit, the category chart too: the record's defaults are `TotalSpend`/`Currency`, so an unstamped series would be wrong); `ChartData.Unit` stays the first measure's. `ChartConfigInput`'s new fields are nullable (absent = none, for an older client). The frontend draws every series that is not a value as a line over the values (`chartFormat.ts` `isValue`, `ChartView`; a year earlier with nothing in it is not drawn, only listed in the table and said in a note), and words a linear trend from its first value to its last (in the chart's units, so mpg reads right), and why a line asked for is missing. The ready-made charts, the key figures and the layout presets live in the frontend (`dashboard/widgets.ts`, pure; the figures drawn by `dashboard/Figures.tsx`) and use the same `vehicleChartData` query. **The dashboard's arrangement** (#211): `DashboardLayoutValues` (tiles `DashboardTile(Id, X, Y, W, H, Pinned)` in a four-column grid, ids a figure's, `preset:<id>` or `chart:<guid>`; the hidden saved charts; shape and bounds only (x + w ≤ 4, h ≤ 24, 60 per list, `dashboard.itemInvalid`/`tooMany`), never overlaps: the web app packs them) in `VehicleDashboardLayouts` (the vehicle's, Edit on its logs, `dashboard.needEditToChange`) and `UserDashboardLayouts` (one's own, View; keyed by user without a foreign key, deleted with the user), both cascading with the vehicle, the tiles as JSON text without a length cap; `DashboardService` drops a saved chart the layout's audience cannot see (another vehicle's, gone, a private one on the vehicle's), never refuses it; GraphQL `Vehicle.dashboard` (nullable, per vehicle: only `VehicleDashboard` selects it, never a list), `saveDashboardLayout`, `resetDashboardLayout`. The web app shows one's own, else the vehicle's, else `LAYOUT_PRESETS.standard` (today's page), through `widgets.ts` `resolveLayout` (merged like a grid's columns: unknown ids dropped, a tile no smaller than its widget's `MIN_SIZE` (a figure stored three rows high before figures took four), a saved chart not mentioned at the first free spot, a built-in not mentioned not shown; `pack` keeps each tile's column and lifts it, pinned ones stay; `flow` lays the reading order into fewer columns for a tablet and a phone and back into four after a save there, each tile at the first place after the one before it, so the order holds (`moveTile` relies on it); a figure tile is four rows, 180 px, what its label, value, hint and sparkline take) and draws it with `DashboardGrid.tsx` (react-grid-layout, see the frontend rule); `DashboardPanel` edits in place (Everyone / Just me, the latter hidden with sign-in off; drag, the corner resize, the tile menu's earlier/later/wider/narrower/taller/shorter/pin/remove (`moveTile`, `resizeTile`, `pinTile`, `removeTile`), Add widget by click (`addTile`, at the bottom) or by dragging in, a preset, reset; Save off while unreachable). **The trend figures** (#211 D): `VehicleSummary.Trends` (`DashboardTrends`: `Spending` and `CostPerDistance` as `CurrencyChange`s per currency, the main currency first and always (also with nothing in the windows, so the figure headlines the card's currency), then those used in either window, the last 12 whole months against the 12 before (this month left out), the latter money per 100 distance units over `DistanceIn` of each window (null with nothing driven); `Consumption` as a `ConsumptionChange` of the mean of the last 10 stored consumptions (the summary's own list, built once) against the 10 before, with both counts (the recent mean as soon as there is one, the earlier from 3 fill-ups on, else null; the tile names the counts, since either side can hold fewer than 10); `Distance` this year to today against last year to the same day, `AddYears(-1)` for a leap day, null for a vehicle without a reading; the costs are listed once and the two windows' distances found once, since every summary works them out), worked out by `StatsCalculator.Summary` from the data it has and selected by `VehicleDashboard` only (never the card fragment). The web app's `Figures.tsx` words a change with an arrow and a per cent (`Change`: "HUF 60,000 (12%) more than the 12 months before", consumption as uses more/less fuel). The ready-made charts `spendSoFar`, `costPerDistance`, `consumptionTrend`, `distanceYearEarlier`, `distanceAndConsumption` and the presets `spending`/`usage` (`presetTiles`) are in `widgets.ts`; Standard does not change, a layout shows only what it names. *Copy as my chart* on a ready-made card opens `ChartBuilderDialog` with `start` (title + recipe). A new figure or ready-made chart = an entry in `widgets.ts` + its texts (+ a `Figure` case); a layout preset = `LAYOUT_PRESETS` + `dashboard.layoutPresets.*`. Add a metric = enum value + calculator case + i18n (`charts.metrics.*`) + builder rules in `dashboard/chartFormat.ts` (`normalise` mirrors `ChartConfig.Validated`).
