# Tyres

- **The model (#287, parts 1–4 of #286):** `Domain/Tyres`.
  - **The name (#288):** a set's time on the car is an *installation* (`TyreInstallation`, table `TyreInstallations`; Hungarian *időszak*), called a stretch before. `RenameTyreInstallations` renamed the table, its keys and indexes, and what was stored as text under the old name: tombstones' `EntityType`, the sync ledger's `Kind`, `ReasonKey` (`tyres.stretch*`) and payload field names (`…TyreStretch`, `stretchId`), and the arguments of the parked changes' notifications (`change`, `reason`). Waiting changes on a device under the old names are not mapped (the owner: a preview's).
  - `TyreSet` (name, season, size, *in use since*, made week and year, the rough figure `RoughDistance` with `RoughUntil`, the first day it does not cover; retired; trashed).
  - `TyreInstallation` (a set's time on the car: `From`, `Until` the day it came off, null while on; `PairOneFront` true/false/null for a whole set's pairs; at least one day; vehicle and owner denormalized, the only cascading key the set's: a key to the vehicle as well would give SQL Server two cascade paths).
  - `TyreEntry` (#288, table `TyreEntries`, replacing the day line `TyreLogDay` by `AddTyreEntries`): a line of the tyre log, its `Kind` (`TyreEntryKind` by name: `Swap`, `PositionsChanged`, `PunctureRepaired`, `ValveReplaced`, `WheelBalancing`, `StorageFee`, `Bought`, `Other`, `Note`), a set or none (`SetId`, no foreign key), its day, the `OdometerReading` it owns (one of the car's readings; a line in the trash puts it in the trash too, so it stops counting until the line comes back) and a note. The vehicle's is the only cascading key. **Every installation has its line**, with the installation's id (`InstallationId` the same): `ForInstallation` makes a *Swap* line, or *Positions changed* when the same set came off that day; its kind and set are locked (`tyres.swapLineLocked`), its day is always the installation's first. The other kinds are added as lines (`Create`; the two of a swap refused, `tyres.entryKindNotAllowed`).
  - **A line's amount** (#288 step 3, the owner's decisions 2, 4, 5): an ordinary `Expense` (its cost, a category, a title the client words), linked by `TyreEntry.ExpenseId` (no foreign key, an index; `AddTyreEntryExpenses`), on the line's day, holding **the line's very reading** (one row, one object: `TyreRepository.LoadAsync` shares it, `Expense.ShareReadingObject`). The reading is live while a live holder has it (`TyreExpenses.Settle`); the expense follows its line before every save (`TyreService.Linked`, `Expense.FollowLine`), except while it lives on with its line in the trash. `ExpenseTrashedWithEntry`: the two went to the trash together (the remove dialog's box, `withExpense`; a swap the history trashes takes its fee along, the owner's choice), so restoring the line brings it back; restoring the expense alone clears it and brings only the expense back. An emptied amount trashes the expense, one typed again restores it; one typed for an expense left without its amount (cleared on the Expenses tab for a photo to give) makes its cost (`FollowLine`, handed to the save as a created cost), its review state as it was. A note takes none (`tyres.noteTakesNoAmount`); a fee where no installation starts that day goes on an `Other` line, and a swap a join moves takes its fee to the new day (the owner's choices). From the Expenses tab (`ExpenseService` → `TyreService.UpdateExpenseAsync`/`TrashExpenseAsync`/`RestoreExpenseAsync`) a linked expense's date and odometer change its line (a swap's moves the swap by `MoveSwap`); while its line is in the trash it is edited alone (a new day or odometer its own, with a reading of its own), and restoring the line then parts the two (`UnlinkExpense`, the owner's choice); an amount, title or note changed alone keeps the pair. A line in the trash never pulls its expense (`Linked`); a swap a join trashes lets go of the reading its kept line took. On the device a waiting edit of a linked expense shows on its line (`replay.ts` `touchesTyres`/`expenseEdited`), and taking back the change that made the expense drops its waiting edits (`changes.ts` `withoutTyreDependents`). A photo's reading on a linked expense becomes the line's (`LogPhotoFiller` → `ExpenseReadingFilledAsync`). A new set's price is a `Bought` line (`NewTyreSet.Price`, `BoughtOn`, `BoughtEntryId`). Both purges keep a reading the other side holds; the expense purge clears the line's link.
  - **The migration** (`AddTyreEntries`, SQL per provider): every installation's line with its id; a day line onto the line of the live installation (of a live set) starting that day, else a *Note* line keeping its id and reading; parked `SetTyreLogDay` changes deleted with the notifications that ask to decide about them (they would name no change and lead to nothing on Waiting to sync), `TyreLogDay` tombstones too; `Down` lossy (a day keeps its first live line's reading and note; the lines' parked changes and their notifications go). Run against PostgreSQL, MySQL and SQL Server containers (#301).
  - All three are `ISynced` and `OfflineEntityType`s; `SyncChangeKind` has a kind per mutation.
- **Worked out on read, nothing stored:** `TyreCalculator` (pure).
  - **The odometer of a day:** that day's highest reading, else a straight line between the nearest readings (approximate), after the latest reading the latest (approximate), before the first unknown.
  - **An installation's distance:** both installations of a boundary day use the same value. An installation still on the car ends at the latest reading, exactly. One before the fuel log counts from its first reading or is `NotMeasured`. Never below 0.
  - **A set's figures:** the rough figure plus its installations, front and rear per pair, and `RoughSuggestion` once installations measure time `RoughUntil` stood for. The suggestion is shown, never applied by itself.
  - **The device's port:** `src/tyres/odometer.ts`, checked on the same cases by `TyreOdometer.unit` (the readings come with `vehicleTyres.readings`). It only words hints and log lines.
- **The rules:** `TyreHistory` (pure).
  - **A swap on a day:** what covers that day ends there; the set runs until the installation it cut would have ended, else the next start. The set's installations join when they meet with the same pairs.
  - **A second swap on a day** (another set's installation starts then) is `tyres.swapExists` (args `date`, `set`) unless `replace`; replacing trashes that installation. The same set on its own first day only sets its pairs.
  - **Change positions:** in place on the installation's first day, else it splits.
  - **A swap moved** (`MoveSwap`, its line's day changed): the installation starts on the new day and the one that came off on its old first day comes off then; at or before that one's own first day `tyres.swapBeforePrevious` (args `set`, `from`).
  - **The lines follow** (`TyreLog.Follow`, pure, after each change): a created installation's line made (`LineFor`), a trashed one's line trashed, a moved one's line moved (its reading with it); an installation a join takes away from its first day (`TyreChanges.JoinedAway`) leaves what its line held (reading, note) on that day, on a new *Note* line of the set (`TyreEntry.Keeping`), never moved or trashed with the join.
  - **The checks** after every change: `tyres.setOnTwice`, `tyres.twoSetsAtOnce`, `tyres.setRetired`; `tyres.restoreConflict` while restoring. *In use since* is month-level and limits nothing.
  - **The retry flags:** `pairsGiven: false` (from papers) leaves a set's pairs; `repeatable` (from the queue only, never online or applied anyway: `SyncGraphQL`'s `resolving`) answers a set already on the car that day with the pairs asked for as a retry (a joined swap kept no id), with other pairs `tyres.alreadyOnCar`; for a change of positions, pairs already as asked on the installation's first day.
- **The service:** `TyreService`.
  - **Access:** the logs' rules through `LogAccessGuard`.
  - **Saves:** every change checked and saved in one transaction (`ITyreRepository.SaveAsync`: rows let go of deleted in a second save).
  - **Readings:** each typed reading is validated by `OdometerService`, the line's own excluded, and the readings of one change must not fall in day order (`odometer.belowPrevious`). What a swap, past swap, change of positions or installation types (a reading, a note) goes on the line of the installation that starts that day; where none does (a join, an installation's last day) on a new *Note* line of the set (`TypedAsync`).
  - **Lines** (`AddEntryAsync`, `UpdateEntryAsync`, `TrashEntryAsync`, `RestoreEntryAsync`): addressed by id; a swap's line and its installation are trashed and restored together (`TrashSwapAsync`/`RestoreSwapAsync`, a restore refused with the history's reason), also from `TrashInstallationAsync`; a new day on a swap's line moves the swap (`MoveSwap`); the installation form's change of *From* moves its line.
  - **Client ids:** a set's, its first installation's, a swap's and a past swap's installation (its line takes the same id), an added line's, so a change sent again changes nothing; an id taken by the other kind (a line's for an installation, or the reverse) is `sync.idTaken`, and so is a new set's `boughtEntryId` a line or an installation has (the add's own repeat is answered by its set's id before). An amount typed on a line that had none makes an expense with the device's `expenseId`: `UpdateEntryAsync` saves it through `SaveOnceAsync`, so the same change saved by another request at once is answered as the repeat (the line names that expense by then).
  - **Past swaps:** at most 25 per change (`tyres.pastSwapsTooMany`), so one fits the sync ledger.
  - **Retiring:** only a set in storage (`tyres.retireOnCar`).
  - **The trash:** a trashed set hides its installations and lines and brings back only those not trashed on their own.
  - **The units lock:** a rough figure locks the vehicle's units (`vehicle.unitsLocked`).
  - **The tyre trash:** sets and lines (a line of no set or of a live set; a swap's row with its installation's `Until`), one query (`TyreRepository.Trash`, `Concat`, the kind by name as a string so both halves agree, checked to translate on the four providers in `OfflineFeedTranslationTests`). It is purged through the change tracker for tombstones: a set with its installations and lines, a line with its installation and reading.
  - **Users:** `UserDataRepository` moves the tyre rows with a user; a vehicle's purge takes them through the cascade.
- **GraphQL:** `vehicleTyres(vehicleId)` (`VehicleTyres`: `TyreSetInfo` with figures and installations with distances, the `entries` newest day first (a swap's line first on its day; a line's `amount`, `currency`, `category` and `expenseTrashed` through `TyreEntryExpenseLoader`), `expenseCategory` (the latest linked expense's, to offer), `readings`), `Expense.tyreEntry` (`TyreEntryRef`, `ExpenseTyreEntryLoader`), the fee (`TyreFeeInput`: `fee` on the line, swap and positions inputs, `price` with `boughtOn` and `boughtEntryId` on a set's; from a device an amount names its `expenseId`), `deleteTyreEntry(input: { id, withExpense })`, `tyreTrash` with its counts (`orderBy: DELETED_AT`, `direction`; rows `SET`/`ENTRY` with `entryKind`, `day`, `until`), and a mutation per change answering with the vehicle's tyres (`addTyreEntry`, `updateTyreEntry`, `deleteTyreEntry`, `restoreTyreEntry` for the lines).
- **Offline (server side):** the feed's first page carries the vehicle's tyres whole (`offlineChanges.tyres`) and no tyre tombstones. A parked tyre change tells its set, installation or line as it is now (`SyncCurrent.tyreSet` / `tyreInstallation` / `tyreEntry`).
- **The device:**
  - **The policies:** `VehicleTyres` is kept (and kept from the feed's first page by `pull.ts`, null too: tyres the reader may no longer see leave the device), `TyreTrash` online only.
  - **Changes offline (part 4, the owner's Q3: shown done, marked):** every tyre mutation goes through `useTyreMutation` → `submitChange` (entity `tyres`, the action the mutation's field, the input the mutation's own, a bare id as `{ id }`; `offline/tyreChanges.ts` says what each targets, creates and needs). Before one is kept the device checks it with `tyres/replay.ts` `applyTyreChange` over what the tab shows (the server's refusal as a `TyreRuleError`, which `keyedOf` and `useErrorText` read, so `tyres.swapExists` asks before it waits). They never fold; a change taken back takes along what was made on what it created; `push.ts` sends one after what it needs, as its own `ChangeInput` field; all 18 kinds are in `SYNC_KINDS`. An older build's `setTyreLogDay` change is left out at load (`outbox.ts` `understood`), and a kept answer with days instead of lines counts as no tyres (`replay.ts` `current`).
  - **The device's answer:** `localResolvers` `VehicleTyres` (`waitingFor` while the vehicle's changes wait) is `replayTyres` over the kept answer, in `seq` order, a change that no longer fits left out; the lines follow the history as the server's (`tyres/tyreLog.ts` `follow`, the join's `joined` hook of `swap`), a line a waiting change made or changed has version 0, a Note line made for what was typed with a swap that made no installation has the swap's `installationId` as the server's `TypedAsync` gives it (so it can be changed while the swap waits), while one whose id only the server chooses (an installation's reading on a day nothing starts, what a joined line held) has the device's own (`<change>:note:<day>`, `<change>:kept:<line>`; `entries.ts` `isDeviceLine`) and is shown without its Edit and bin until synced: a change naming that id could never be sent, and the car's readings are the server's less the lines' as they were plus the lines' as they are; what only the server works out stays as it was for what did not change (a new or changed installation's distance `NOT_MEASURED`), and a set or installation a waiting change changed has version 0, so the next change of it goes without a version. `usePendingTyres` (the same replay) marks the touched cards *New* / *Changed · not synced* and their distances *updated once synced*. `LogDefaults` and an added vehicle's odometer figure count the tyre log's readings.
  - **The port of the rules:** `tyres/tyreHistory.ts` (with `moveSwap`) and `tyres/tyreLog.ts`, pinned to `TyreHistory` and `TyreLog` by `tests/backend/contracts/tyre-swaps/tyre-swaps.json` (`TyreSwapContractTests`, `TyreHistory.unit`; `lines`, `trashedLines`, `lineNotes`, the `move` op): change one, change the other and the cases.
  - **Waiting to sync:** `sync.kinds.tyres.*`, named by the set and day; no Edit (the owner's decision); a swap parked with `tyres.swapExists` offers *Replace the swap* (`resolveSyncChange` with `replace: true`) in place of *Apply anyway*. Rows found on the device after a restore (`foundRows.ts`) leave tyres out.
  - **UI offline:** `OfflineNote` atop the tyre dialogs; `useTyreToasts` says a kept save is saved on the device, and its Undo takes a kept change back instead of sending the opposite one.
- **UI:**
  - **The tab:** `pages/vehicle/TyresPanel.tsx` (lazy), between *Recurring* and *Details*. It shows the sets on the car, in storage and retired, *Add entry* beside *Add a tyre set*, and the tyre log (#288): a `ul` of the lines, each a button named "{kind}, {set}, {day}" (the day bold, the kind a chip, the set, then the reading and the note; a swap's line without a typed reading the day's odometer worked out, ≈, or *before the fuel log*) that opens *Edit entry*, and its bin (*Move to the trash: {kind}, {day}*).
  - **The dialogs** in `src/tyres/`:
    - `TyreSetDialog` (add; with `edit`, a set's own details, opened by *Edit set* in its history: no *Where are they now?* or *These tyres are new*);
    - `SwapDialog` (swap, or `rotate`: the pairs as `Segmented` and an `aria-hidden` picture);
    - `PastSwapsDialog`;
    - `HistoryDialog`, with the installation form and `RoughDialog`; an installation's bin is its line's (`TrashEntryDialog`);
    - `EntryDialog` (add or edit a line: *What was done* and *Set* as `LabeledSelect`s, the swap kinds not offered, the day, the reading, the note; a swap's line with its kind and set disabled, why in their hints, and its day moving the swap) and `TrashEntryDialog` (*Move to the trash?*: the line in a sentence, what a swap's line takes along, a reading that stops counting, *It can be restored from the trash*; Undo restores it); the words in `tyres/entries.ts` (`ENTRY_KINDS`, `isSwapLine`, `useEntryWords`).
  - **Mutations:** each goes through `useTyreMutation`, which, when sent, writes the answer into `VehicleTyres` at once and asks `VehicleTyres` (so the device's kept answer follows), `VehicleDetails` and `LogDefaults` again.
  - **Replacing:** `tyres.swapExists` turns the dialog's button into *Replace the swap* and sends again with `replace`; changing the day, the set or the rows asks afresh.
  - **Opening:** a dialog that keeps state while closed (add set, swap, past swaps) sets it afresh when it opens, from the tyres as they are then (the edit form from the set it edits).
  - **The Trash page:** a *Tyres* tab, `TyreTrash`: sets and lines ("Tyre swap: {set}", a swap's *Swap* chip and "{day} · on the car until {until}" / "· on the car now"), a line restored with `restoreTyreEntry` ("Restored, with its expense." when `withExpense`); restoring a linked expense on the Expenses tab whose line is in the trash says the line stays there.
  - **Amounts in the UI** (#288 step 3): `FeeFields` (*Paid* / *Paid for the swap* / *Price*, the currency, *Category* prefilled with `expenseCategory` or *Tyres*/*Gumik*; `feeOf` reads them, the title `useEntryWords().feeTitle`) in `EntryDialog` (none for a note), `SwapDialog` and `TyreSetDialog` (a used set's *Bought on* once a price is typed); a line shows its amount (and names it); `TrashEntryDialog` asks with a box (ticked) whether the expense goes too (`ConfirmDialog`'s body slot). On the Expenses tab a linked expense has the *Tyre log* chip (*· line in the trash*), its edit dialog `TyreLogBanner` with *Open in the tyre log* (`?tab=tyres&entry=<id>` opens the line's dialog), its trash confirmation says the line stays. Offline, the replay carries a line's amount, and `localResolvers` `withChanges` shows the expense a waiting amount makes as a new row with its line (`tyreChanges.ts` `tyreFeeExpense`, marked by `outbox.markOf`); over an expense the device holds already it lays only what follows the line (day, reading, amount, currency, category), never its title or note.
  - **Texts:** `tyres.*` (the lines' `tyres.entries.*`), `trash.tyres.*` and `errors.tyres.*` in English and Hungarian.
